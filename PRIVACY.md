# Inkshot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Inkshot collects no data. It contains no networking code, so nothing you capture,
record or type ever leaves your Mac.

- **No data collection.** Inkshot has no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting,
  advertising or accounts, and it never connects to the internet.
- **Screen Recording permission.** macOS asks for this so Inkshot can capture your
  screen. Inkshot only captures when you start a capture or recording yourself,
  and it never records audio.
- **Your files stay local.** Captures are copied to your clipboard and saved to a
  folder you choose. A history of your last 60 captures is kept in Inkshot's own
  folder on your Mac and pruned automatically.
- **Settings** such as shortcuts and preferences are stored locally by macOS.
- **No third parties.** Inkshot shares nothing with anyone, because it has nothing
  to share.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/amoswzw/InkShot/issues.
